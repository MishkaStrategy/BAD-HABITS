# Sources

Research performed for the BAD HABITS / Digital After Dark proposal. Accessed 2026-08-15 unless noted otherwise.

## BAD HABITS — official/public information

- Official network site: https://badhabitslounge.ru/
  - Network positioning, 5 Novosibirsk locations, public phone numbers, menu/special-offer context and catering.
- Official branch page — Большевистская, 94: https://badhabitslounge.ru/bolshevistskaya
  - Opening hours, menu/booking links, branch-specific special offers and 18+ notice.
- 2GIS — Большевистская, 94: https://2gis.ru/novosibirsk/firm/70000001083704156
  - Branch card, address, public rating context, booking/contact links.
- 2GIS reviews — Большевистская, 94: https://2gis.ru/novosibirsk/firm/70000001083704156/tab/reviews
  - Research themes: daylight/panoramic windows, calm atmosphere, remote-work use, service, food, hookah product, VIP room and group visits.
- 2GIS branch list: https://2gis.ru/novosibirsk/branches/70000001039522665
  - Current five-location network structure used in the branch-selector concept.

## Architecture / interior research

- GB | Architects — BADHABITS project: https://gb-arch.ru/projects/dizajn-proekt-kalyannoj/
  - 116 m² project, concrete, warm light, street art/graffiti/calligraphy, curved ceiling light, illuminated glass blocks.
- WA Journal — “Bad Habits: бетон и неон в Новосибирске”: https://wajournal.ru/interiors/kafe-i-restorany/beton-i-neon-v-novosibirske-otkrylas-kalyannaya-bad-habits/
  - 45-person capacity, main lounge, kitchen, bar and VIP room; navigation role of the curved light; custom furniture; photo credit Наталья Бочкова.
- myDecor — “Бетон и граффити: бар Bad Habits в Новосибирске”: https://mydecor.ru/interior-hotels-restaurants/beton-i-graffiti-bar-bad-habits-v-novosibirske/
  - Architect commentary on eclectic industrial materiality, warm light, street art and a deliberately non-cold atmosphere.
